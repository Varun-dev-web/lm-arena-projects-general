## Why these five years

The five most recent **completed** October/November examination series for which
0625 question papers are available are **2025, 2024, 2023, 2022 and 2021**.
The Oct/Nov 2026 series is still in progress at the date above, so it is excluded.
Cambridge's own "Syllabus component weighting factors" document lists
`November 2025 · 0625 · 41 / 42 / 43`, confirming all three components were set
in the November 2025 series — [3](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/images/206341-syllabus-component-weighting-factors.pdf).
Components **41, 42, 43** are the three variants of **Paper 4 (Theory, Extended)** —
[3](https://educateachange.com/past-papers/igcse/physics-0625/2025/oct-nov/) and
[2](https://lumiexams.com/igcse/physics-0625/2025/).

## Where the links come from

Cambridge International's public past-papers page for 0625 offers only a small
selection (June 2024 papers plus 2023 specimen papers) and states: *"This is only
a selection of our papers. Registered Cambridge International Schools can access
a wide range of teaching and learning materials and examination resources through
our School Support Hub"* — [1](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-physics-0625/past-papers/).
The School Support Hub is a login-only service for registered schools —
[3](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/417125-cambridge-school-support-brochure.pdf).
I did **not** attempt to access it. **No official public Cambridge download link
exists for these 15 Oct/Nov question papers**, so every link below is hosted by
**PapaCambridge** (`pastpapers.papacambridge.com`), a third-party archive. These
files are **not** official Cambridge-hosted files.

Each "Download" link below is PapaCambridge's own `Download File` endpoint taken
directly from that year's live landing page. Every one of the 15 entries was
checked on the live landing page for the exact label ("November 20XX Question
Paper 41/42/43") and the exact filename (`0625_wXX_qp_4Y.pdf`). Cross-checks with
independent archives (e.g. [6](https://xtrapapers.co/papers/caie/igcse/physics-0625/2025-oct-nov),
[10](https://pastpapers.co/caie/igcse/physics-0625/2025-oct-nov),
[1](https://www.learnedguys.com/home/document_library/view/1742),
[1](https://studyhatch.com/Past-Papers/Subject?code=0625)) confirm the same
filenames exist for those sessions.

> download the PDF files themselves (as requested), so I cannot confirm each
