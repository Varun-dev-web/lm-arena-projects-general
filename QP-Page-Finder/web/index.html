What this app does & does not do

Does

  • Read PDFs locally with bundled pdf.js text extraction plus offline OCR
  • Report filename, component, question number, physical PDF page/range and a position-verified printed page
  • Flag uncertain matches and let you mark them relevant / not relevant
  • Open the original PDF at the matching page in a built-in viewer
  • NEW: auto-render a QP capture and a matching MS capture for each confirmed question, let you align both crops, compile two separate A4 PDFs, preview / print them, and download each after your approval
  • Read PapaCambridge MS filenames and report the exact MS PDF page for each selected question number

Does not

  • Upload, transmit or send your PDFs, their text or your captures anywhere
  • Bypass PDF passwords, encryption or access restrictions
  • Modify, overwrite, split or combine your original PDFs (the A4 file is a brand-new PDF you create)
  • Show question text in the results table, or generate answers / mark-scheme text (it only handles your own local MS PDFs)
  • Merge QP and MS pages into one PDF, or download anything without your explicit approval in the preview
