What this app does & does not do

Does

  • Read PDFs locally with bundled pdf.js text extraction
  • Use bundled offline OCR (Tesseract WebAssembly + English data) for scanned pages
  • Report filename, component, question number, physical PDF page/range and printed page when reliable
  • Flag uncertain matches and let you mark them relevant / not relevant
  • Open the original PDF at the matching page in a built-in viewer

Does not

  • Upload, transmit or send your PDFs or their text anywhere
  • Bypass PDF passwords, encryption or access restrictions
  • Make screenshots, export pages, create or combine PDFs
  • Reproduce question text or produce answers / mark schemes
  • Modify your original PDFs in any way
