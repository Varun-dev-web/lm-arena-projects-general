## Notes on verification

- **Syllabus / session / component** for every row above was confirmed by reading the mirror's own session index for `0625 / {year} / October-November`, e.g. [2023 Oct/Nov](https://pastpapers.co/caie/igcse/physics-0625/2023-oct-nov).
- **Document type** (QP vs MS) is encoded in the Cambridge filename convention: `0625_w<yy>_qp_<component>` / `0625_w<yy>_ms_<component>`.
- **Page counts could not be collected**: the sandbox this work ran in permits outbound network access only to github.com, npm and PyPI, so the PDFs could not be fetched. `assemble_0625_octnov.py` prints and records the real page counts when you run it on a normal connection.
- **Official Cambridge route:** teachers at registered Cambridge schools can download these exact documents from the [School Support Hub](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-physics-0625/past-papers/) (login required). That is the recommended source: it is authorised, complete and correctly versioned.
- Cambridge's stated position: teachers may download and print past papers for use with their students, but the materials **may not be published or re-sold without prior approval** ([source](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/115004333125-Can-I-reproduce-Cambridge-past-examination-papers-for-use-in-the-classroom-with-my-students)).
