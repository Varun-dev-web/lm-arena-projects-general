Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625) — October/November 2021–2025
Components 41, 42, 43 (Paper 4, Extended Theory) — QP + MS = 30 files
=====================================================================

Download these 30 documents from the Cambridge School Support Hub
(https://schoolsupporthub.cambridgeinternational.org/ — login required,
available to staff at registered Cambridge schools).

Cambridge's own files normally use exactly these names, so if you keep the
default filenames the assembler finds them instantly. If your downloads come
out with different names, that is fine too — the assembler reads each PDF's
own printed header and matches it up automatically.

Notes
-----
- "w" = the October/November (winter) series. Make sure you do NOT download
  the s (May/June) or m (March) versions — the assembler rejects them, but
  it is quicker to get it right at download time.
- Component 41/42/43 are the three regional variants of Paper 4. All three
  are listed here because all three were requested.
- Do not substitute the Confidential Instructions (0625_w*_ci_5*.pdf) for
  any of these; they belong to Paper 5/6, not Paper 4.

Then run:
    python3 assemble_0625_octnov.py --from-dir ./papers
