### Scope decisions (stated explicitly rather than guessed)

- `0625/41/42/42` contains a duplicated `42`. I have used **41 / 42 / 43**, the
  three Paper 4 variants used in the earlier 15-row report and on the live pages.
- "Past 5 years" is read as **2021–2025**, the same five years as the original
  Oct/Nov list. Because 2026 March and May/June papers are now also on the site, a
  clearly-labelled **2026 supplement** is appended below, so the 2022–2026 reading
  is covered too. Oct/Nov 2026 is advertised only as *predictions*, NOT a past paper,
  and is deliberately excluded.
- "All series" = **Feb/March (`m`)**, **May/June (`s`)** and **Oct/Nov (`w`)**.
- Verified fact: for 0625, the March series is published **only as variant 42**
  (plus 12 / 22 / 32 / 52 / 62). There are **no 41 or 43 March papers or mark
  schemes**. Those cells are marked MISSING rather than invented.

### How the app uses these names

The rebuilt QP Page Finder has two independent folder bars. It parses a filename such
as `0625_w21_ms_41.pdf` into syllabus `0625`, series `w21` (2021 Oct/Nov), doc type
`ms`, component `41`, matches it to `0625_w21_qp_41.pdf`, then indexes question
numbers inside the MS to report the exact physical MS PDF page for each ✓ Relevant
question. QP and MS pages are compiled, previewed, printed and downloaded as **two
separate A4 PDFs**, never one combined file.
